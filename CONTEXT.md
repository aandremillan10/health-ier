# Clinic records

The language used for the clinic's records and staff responsibilities.

## Language

**Discipline**:
A clinical area of work, such as medical, dental, or medical technology.

**Medical chart**:
The patient's clinical record used for medical care, separate from the dental chart.

**Dental chart**:
The patient's clinical record used for dental care, separate from the medical chart.

**Authored entry**:
A contribution to a clinical record attributed to the staff member who wrote it.

**Clinic head**:
One of the clinic's medical doctors with added oversight access to read records across disciplines; this designation is separate from their medical role.

**Patient registry**:
The shared list of patient identities used by all clinic disciplines.
_Avoid_: Medical chart, dental chart

**Draft entry**:
An authored clinical contribution that remains editable by its author before explicit finalization.

**Finalized entry**:
An authored clinical contribution whose original content is preserved after the author finalizes it.

**Correction**:
An attributed change that preserves and refers to the original finalized entry.
_Avoid_: Overwrite

**Vital signs**:
The patient measurements recorded by the nursing attendant before the medical consultation.

**Medical consultation**:
The authored medical chart entry containing a clinician's assessment of the patient on a consultation date.

**Prescription**:
A clinician-issued document recording medication instructions for the patient.

**Medical certificate**:
A clinician-issued document recording the patient's consultation findings for a stated purpose.

**Nursing attendant**:
The clinic staff role identified separately from the medical, dental, medical technology, and clerk roles.

**Clerk**:
The clinic staff role identified separately from the clinical staff roles.
